Engineering extensions and explicit limitations:
- Ground/base inclination factors use the USACE Meyerhof-based family; not a full AASHTO/FHWA implementation.
- Layered bearing is a simplified horizontal-layer 2V:1H screening model; not arbitrary inclined interfaces or full numerical failure mechanisms.
- Seismic checks do not replace full site-specific liquefaction, cyclic strength-loss, residual-strength profiles, seismic settlement, lateral-spreading or time-history analysis.
- Sliding Rankine auto mode assumes uniform backfill and level backfill surface. Complex wall friction, sloping ground, layered backfill and hydraulic pressure require separate evaluation.
- Bishop automatic search is limited to homogeneous soil, circular slip surfaces and horizontal groundwater; no layered soil, non-circular surfaces, surcharge, seismic or 3D effects. Refine search grids and cross-check with Spencer/Morgenstern-Price for design.
- Manual Bishop slice mode requires user-supplied geometry and material values for one consistent circular slip surface.
