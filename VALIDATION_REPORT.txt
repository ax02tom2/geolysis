USACE 2025 gross equivalent bearing pressure and actual bearing safety check — PR #19

- Implemented USACE EM 1110-1-1905 (31 Jul 2025) Eqs. 5-2/5-3 and 5-4 to 5-8: qeq = V/A′ − uD for isolated spread footings and qeq = V/B′ − uD for continuous strip footings; eccentric effective dimensions B′ and L′ are used.
- Implemented Eq. 5-23: actual FS_bearing = qult,gross / qeq. The minimum bearing-capacity FS is 3.0 per Table 2-1. The UI separately checks the strict middle-third eccentricity limits eB < B/6 and eL < L/6; equality at the boundary is rejected.
